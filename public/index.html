Displays vs. actuates

A weather API answers a person. Senlay answers a system.

When software shows a number to a human, the human applies their own judgement. When software acts on a number, nobody does. Senlay supplies the missing scepticism: provenance, freshness, confidence, source disagreement, and an explicit statement of what the system does not know.

Why Senlay exists

Agents should not guess when the world can be measured.

Training data can describe a place. A generic API can return a number. Senlay adds the missing trust layer: what was observed, where it came from, when it was measured, whether sources agree, and how much the result should influence a real-world decision.

Sense

Bring fragmented signals together.

Hardware sensors, public networks, forecast providers, satellite events, terrain, marine feeds, GPS tracks, and customer IoT devices enter through one integration layer.

Verify

Attach evidence to every answer.

Observations and predictions stay distinct. Freshness, proximity, confidence, source health, and model-versus-reality disagreement remain visible instead of being hidden.

Interpret

Return context an agent can act on.

Field-specific rules turn raw readings into structured context, risk events, and plain-language uncertainty without pretending the API is a final safety authority.

Contribute or pay

Coverage comes from participants. Revenue comes from systems that act.

The two sides do not compete for the same budget. Contributors pay in data and receive the network; consumers pay in cash because they will never install a weather station.

Contribute

Attach a sensor, get the whole network.

A weather station, an anemometer, a tide gauge, a marina, a kite station, a farm probe. Attach it directly and receive verified reasoning across every instrument around you, not just your own readings. No cash, no token.

Reciprocate

Bring a network you already run.

Operators with existing sensor fleets connect them and gain the verification layer plus coverage they do not own. The fleet becomes more valuable because it is reasoned over, not merely read.

Consume

Systems that act, pay cash.

Drone and BVLOS operators, robotics fleets, lifting and construction, parametric insurers, marine operations. They need the answer, not the network, and they are the revenue.

Local observation memory

The API gets better at a place the more it is asked about it.

Every request stores an observation snapshot for that coordinate. From live traffic, Senlay builds its own 7-day local rhythm — typical daily low and peak, ramp start time, recurring gust factor, and domain-specific thresholds. This history is generated by usage and cannot be bought from any upstream data vendor.

Snapshot

Every request is remembered

Each call stores an observation snapshot for its coordinate, so the record of a place deepens every time it is queried.

Rhythm

A 7-day local pattern

Typical daily low and peak, ramp start time, recurring gust factor, and domain thresholds — learned from real traffic, not a generic model.

Not for sale

61 snapshots beat 2

A coordinate with 61 stored snapshots returns local pattern memory; a coordinate with 2 returns model history only. No upstream vendor can sell that memory back to you.

Explicit uncertainty

It tells you what it does not know.

When validated bathymetry is unavailable, Senlay disables seafloor and shallow-water hazard inference and says so in the response. Elevation is never presented as ocean depth. Official alert feeds returning nothing are reported as “no events returned”, never as a safety clearance.

Systems that interpolate across their gaps are the ones that get people hurt. In a safety-critical workflow, an inferred value presented as a measurement is worse than no value.

One core, many fields

A horizontal sensing layer with domain-aware outputs.

The evidence contract stays consistent while the sources, rules, and decision context adapt to each physical workflow.

Autonomous and drone operations

Wind, gusts, precipitation, visibility, terrain exposure, airspace weather, and go/no-go context. BVLOS flight requires a documented pre-flight risk assessment; a provenance trail is a regulatory artifact, not a feature.

Marine and water safety

Waves, tides, current, buoy observations, wind direction, coastal geometry, GPS drift, and rescue-margin context — with SmartSurf as the proving ground.

Lifting and construction

Crane wind limits are a legal threshold. Whether one was exceeded is settled by source and timestamp, not by a nearby forecast.

Parametric insurance

A payout triggered by an environmental threshold must be both provable and contestable. Evidence with provenance and freshness is the record that settles it.

Agriculture and field work

Heat, humidity, evapotranspiration, frost, precipitation, and spray-window conditions, using the ET₀ and VPD calculators already in the modifier engine.

Environmental monitoring

Air quality, fire and hazard feeds, satellite events, earthquakes, volcanoes, and source freshness.

Connected products and IoT

Private station networks, trackers, device telemetry, customer sensors, webhooks, and normalized evidence records.

AI assistants and workflows

One fast-growing segment of the same layer: agent-ready context strings, versioned JSON, API-key access, model integrations, and auditable risk events.

How it works

From unreliable upstreams to explainable agent context.

Senlay is an end-to-end production system, not a static concept page. It handles provider integrations, normalization, trust logic, authentication, persistence, API delivery, and Linux deployment behind a public HTTPS service.

01 · Integrate

Source adapters

Connect weather, marine, aviation, environmental, satellite, and private-device APIs with bounded timeouts and explicit licensing controls.

02 · Normalize

Evidence contracts

Convert incompatible payloads into versioned observations, predictions, static context, provenance, and source-health records.

03 · Reason

Context and risk

Fuse local evidence, terrain modifiers, fallback policy, domain rules, and disagreement into machine- and human-readable outputs.

04 · Operate

Production delivery

Serve through Node/Express, SQLite, Nginx, PM2 or Docker with secrets isolation, health checks, logs, backups, and deployment runbooks.
