Questions

When will paid plans launch?

We're in open beta through Q2 2026. Paid plans will launch once we've validated the platform with real developer usage. All beta users will get early access pricing.

Will free tier always exist?

Yes. A free tier with reasonable limits will always be available. We believe developers should be able to test and prototype without a credit card.

What counts as an API call?

One call to /api/v1/pwm = one API call. This returns the full Physical World Model — 21 sensor sources, early modifier demos, source/freshness metadata, and the context_string ready for any LLM.

Can I use Senlay commercially during beta?

Yes. The beta is fully functional and you're welcome to integrate it into products. Just know that during beta, we may have occasional downtime for upgrades. Paid plans will include SLAs.

Need higher limits or pilot access?

If you are evaluating Senlay for an agent product, research workflow, or enterprise integration, contact us for beta limits, pilot support, and launch pricing.
